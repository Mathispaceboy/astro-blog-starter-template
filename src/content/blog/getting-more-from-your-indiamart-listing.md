Almost every manufacturer and trader in Coimbatore already has an IndiaMART listing, paid or free. Far fewer are getting real value out of it. This guide is about the difference between having a listing and having a listing that actually produces qualified leads.

## Start with what's actually there today

Before changing anything, look at the listing the way a stranger would: search for the product category plus "Coimbatore" (or the relevant city) and see where the listing shows up, what the first product photo looks like, whether the company description is generic boilerplate or says something specific, and whether contact response has been fast or slow over the last month. This ten-minute audit usually explains most of a "leads have dried up" complaint on its own.

## The catalog is doing more work than the profile page

Buyers on IndiaMART browse products, not company pages, most of the time. A catalog with clear individual listings per product — proper photos, specifications filled in completely (not left blank because "the buyer will ask"), and pricing indicated even approximately where competitors are showing it — consistently outperforms a catalog with a handful of generic entries and a request to "contact for details." Every blank specification field is a buyer who moves to the next listing instead of asking a question.

## TrustSEAL and verification badges are worth taking seriously

Buyers filter by verified/TrustSEAL sellers more than most sellers assume, particularly for higher-value B2B purchases where a bad vendor choice is costly. If verification isn't complete, that's usually the highest-leverage single fix available before touching anything else.

## Lead response time matters more than lead volume

A faster first response consistently converts better than a slower one, even when the volume of inbound leads is identical — buyers on IndiaMART are typically messaging or calling several suppliers in the same category simultaneously, and the first credible response often gets the conversation. If leads are coming in but not converting, response time (and who on the team owns picking them up) is worth checking before assuming the leads themselves are low quality.

## On paid plans

IndiaMART offers several paid tiers above the free listing — broadly, options that increase weekly/monthly lead volume, expand catalog size, add features like call forwarding, a custom domain, or corporate email, and (at the higher "Verified Exporter" tiers) target international buyer leads specifically. Reported approximate pricing from current sellers ranges from roughly ₹3,000/month for entry-level paid tiers up to several lakh per year for top exporter tiers — but again, these numbers move, aren't published officially, and should be confirmed directly with IndiaMART before budgeting against them.

The decision on whether a paid tier is worth it should follow this logic: if the free listing, fully optimized (complete catalog, verification, fast response), is already producing leads but the volume is the constraint, a paid tier that increases lead volume can make sense. If the free listing isn't converting the leads it already gets, paying for more volume of the same low-converting leads usually isn't the fix — the catalog and response process need attention first.

## A simple monthly habit

Once a listing is in reasonable shape, the biggest ongoing lever is just consistency: adding new products as they come up, updating specifications when they change, and checking response times monthly rather than treating the listing as something set up once and forgotten. Directories reward active accounts with better placement over time, and buyers can tell the difference between a listing that's clearly maintained and one that hasn't been touched in a year.
